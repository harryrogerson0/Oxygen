Help Page

Aim:
Increase your oxygen level as high as you can. The rest is up to you...

Main Controls:

  • Move left - a
  • Move right - d
  • Jump - w
  • Pause game - Escape
  • Drop items - CLICK on item in inventory
  • Pick up items - CLICK on item on ground
  • Change inventory slots - press keys 1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8,9 or 0
  • Use seletected inventory item or interact with structure - CLICK & hold on ground or structure

Things to do:

  • Take apples off trees
  • Plant saplings with apples
  • Grow saplings with water buckets
  • Grow apples on trees with water buckets
  • Chop down trees with axes
  • Dig holes with spades
  • Fill up buckets with water from holes
  • Put water in holes with water buckets
  • Fill in holes with mud
  • Make mounds with mud
  • Drink water buckets
  • Eat apples
  • Build wigwams, sheds, huts, houses and windmills with wood
  • Knock down wigwams, sheds, huts, houses and windmills with sledgehammers
  • Get sledgehammers, spades, axes and buckets from windmills & factories
  • Knock down factories with sledgehammers
  • Fix broken windmills with wood
  • Drop items

Other general information:

  • Factories and windmills have a cooldown of 60 seconds after use.
  • Player-created structures need a certain amount of space around them.
  • You cannot chop down the last tree.
  • You cannot knock down the last factory / windmill.
